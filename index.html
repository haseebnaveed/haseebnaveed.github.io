Quixel, then Epic Games2019 – 2024

Megascans

Megascans is a library of 18,000+ scanned assets that studios like Rockstar, Ubisoft and Disney use in their games and films. I spent five years on it: running the catalogue at Quixel, staying on through the Epic acquisition, helping launch Bridge and then repricing the whole thing for Fab.

  • 7K → 18Klistings
  • +109%downloads
  • 83.5%CSAT

The home page

The front door to the whole library. For two years I decided every detail about what went on it, picking every row and every feature by hand while the catalogue grew from 7,000 to 18,000+ assets. Behind it sat the asset taxonomy I rebuilt from scratch, so every one of those assets ended up somewhere people would actually look for it.

Collections

Collections bundled assets by theme, from coastlines and castle ruins to a medieval banquet, for people making games, films, archviz and everything in between. I was the only curator, so I picked and ordered every one of them and built the taxonomy they sit in. Hover the names to see a few, click the picture to look inside.
